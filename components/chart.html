Chart

A transaction line-chart card that plots a week of activity (Sun–Thu) as a smooth spline with per-point value labels, faint vertical gridlines and a soft area gradient. Four data states carry meaning through colour — Earn (gain green), Loss (danger red), Flat (brand), and an empty None — all sourced from the --Line-Chart/* tokens. Brand re-themes to lime in dark.

Anatomy

Title + period dropdown over a 362px surface, then a five-point smooth line with value labels (--medium/xs, 10px), gridlines, gradient fill and the Sun–Thu axis at 40% opacity.

Transaction — 362px · smooth spline · 5 points · gradient fillUses --Line-Chart/line-gain · --background/bg-default · --border/subtle

Data states

The same card carries four states. Earn trends up in gain green, Loss trends down in danger red, Flat holds the brand colour, and None is the empty placeholder before data loads.

In dark

On a dark canvas the surface and axis invert; gain stays green and loss stays red, while the brand line re-themes to lime.

Dark — gain green · loss red · brand → lime
